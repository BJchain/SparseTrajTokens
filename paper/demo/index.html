Trajectory Demo Reel

Nine Stereo4D clips from the qualitative figures (Fig. 4 and Fig. 4b), with what the data pipeline extracts drawn into every frame: the outline of each moving object and the world-frame trajectory of its keypoints, projected with the ground-truth camera. Trajectory colour is time, violet at the start and red at the end. Each clip is paired with the TrajQA-B question used in the figure and the verbatim outputs of both models.

About these renders

  • Masks come from SAM 3 re-run with the production settings. Outlines are drawn for the moving objects only; static objects stay unmarked.
  • Trajectories are the down-sampled keypoints of the production final representation, in the world frame, projected into each frame with the ground-truth camera. Non-rigid keypoints are drawn only while visible; rigid ones only where the SE(3) fit is valid. On a moving camera a world-frame path looks stretched in the image, which is expected.
  • Every frame is the same render the paper figures use. The script that made these videos re-draws the figure frames and checks them pixel for pixel against the figure files.
  • Videos play at each clip's real sampling rate (5 fps for eight clips, 15 fps for Carried object), so the motion on screen matches the metres per second in the questions. The highlighted band on the timeline is the interval the question asks about; the small ticks are the frames printed in the paper figure.
  • Questions come from the evaluation splits (test and held-out test). Both fine-tuned models see 8 frames sampled uniformly over the whole clip; ours additionally reads the trajectory tokens. Zero-shot Qwen3-VL-8B answers with a letter only.
  • The motion caption under each clip is generated by rules from the ground-truth tracks. It is not a model output.
  • For the video editor: paper/demo_web/videos/<clip>_{input,masks,traj,both,sbs,tiles}.mp4 (512×512 single layers, sbs = input | masks + trajectories side by side, tiles = the 2×2 sheet this page shows).
